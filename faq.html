FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the BNB Airdrop 2025?

The BNB Airdrop 2025 is an official Web3 campaign by Binance to distribute 1,000 BNB to active community members. Participation is free, but a 0.02 BNB activation fee is required to prevent bots.

How do I claim my BNB?

Connect your wallet, pay the 0.02 BNB activation fee, complete 3 simple tasks (follow, retweet, join Telegram), and your reward will be sent instantly to your wallet.

Is there KYC?

No. This is a fully decentralized airdrop. No personal information is required. Your wallet address is your identity.

When will I receive my BNB?

Most users receive their BNB within 2 minutes of completing the tasks. Transactions are processed on-chain via smart contract.

Which wallets are supported?

MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Coinbase Wallet, SafePal, TokenPocket, and all Web3-compatible wallets on desktop and mobile.

Can I participate from my phone?

Yes. The airdrop is fully mobile-optimized. Just open the site in your phone’s browser and connect using Trust Wallet or MetaMask.
